About Meriem

Meriem Hasker

the best person

Meriem, there are people who pass through your life and people who become part of it, and somehow you managed to become the second without me even noticing when it happened. I think about the way you show up for the people you love, the way your laugh fills a room, the way you make ordinary days feel like something worth remembering. You have a kind of light that I don't think you fully see in yourself, but I do, and I have from the very beginning.

I won't pretend everything between us has been easy, there were moments that quietly broke something in me, and I carried that weight longer than I probably should have. But even through that, what I feel for you never really left, it just waited. Today isn't about any of that though, it's about celebrating you, exactly as you are, and reminding you that no matter what, you will always be my favorite person.
